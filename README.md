# SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM

## Waiting Has Become Part of Everyday Life

Millions of people spend valuable time waiting for services that could be better organized.

Common challenges include:

- Long queues at hospitals and clinics.
- Unpredictable waiting times at barber shops and salons.
- Poor appointment management in service businesses.
- Lack of information about available service times.
- Lost productivity caused by unnecessary physical waiting.

**The Real Problem:** People cannot easily know when they will receive a service or how long they must wait.

---

# SLIDE 3 — OUR SOLUTION

## Introducing SAFIR

SAFIR is a digital platform that connects customers with service providers through smart appointment booking and digital queue management.

Our solution allows customers to:

- Book appointments from anywhere.
- Join digital queues without standing physically in line.
- View their position in a queue.
- Receive notifications when their turn approaches.
- Find available service providers.

**Our Mission:** Give people more control over their time while helping businesses serve customers more efficiently.

---

# SLIDE 4 — HOW SAFIR WORKS

## A Simple Five-Step Experience

**Step 1: Discover**

Customers search for nearby service providers.

**Step 2: Select**

They choose a hospital, clinic, barber shop, salon, or other participating business.

**Step 3: Book or Join**

They select an available appointment or receive a digital queue ticket.

**Step 4: Track**

They monitor their queue position and estimated waiting time.

**Step 5: Get Served**

They receive a notification when their turn approaches and visit the service provider.

**Result:** A more organized and convenient service experience.

# SLIDE 7 — BUSINESS MODEL

## How SAFIR Makes Money

SAFIR uses a business-to-business and business-to-customer revenue model.

**1. Monthly Subscriptions**

Businesses pay monthly to access advanced queue and appointment management tools.

**2. Booking Fees**

SAFIR may charge a small service fee for selected paid bookings.

**3. Premium Business Plans**

Advanced features such as analytics, multiple staff accounts, and automated reporting are offered through premium plans.

**4. Enterprise Solutions**

Large organizations can pay for customized queue management systems.

**Revenue Strategy:** Build recurring revenue through business subscriptions while keeping basic customer access affordable.
